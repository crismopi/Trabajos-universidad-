L153:  L154: # EAC at a Glance
L155: 
L156: * * *
L157: 
L158: The East African Community (EAC) is a regional intergovernmental organisation of eight (8) Partner States, comprising the Republic of Burundi, Democratic Republic of Congo, Republic of Kenya, Republic of Rwanda, Federal Republic of Somalia, Republic of South Sudan, Republic of Uganda and United Republic of Tanzania, with its headquarters in Arusha, Tanzania.
L159: The Federal Republic of Somalia was admitted into the EAC bloc on 24th November, 2023 and became a full member on 4th March, 2024.

L246: EAC VISION 2050 - Pillars & Goals
L247: 
L248: ###  L249: 
L250: Access to afforable and efficient transport, energy, and communication for increased regional Competitiveness.
L251: 
L252:  L253: ###  L254: 
L255: Enhanced Agricultural productivity for food security and transformed rural economy.
L256: 
L257:  L258: 
L259: ###  L260: 
L261: Structural tranformation of the industrial and manufacturing sector through value addition and product deversification based on comparative advantage for regional competitive advantage.
L262: 
L263:  L264: ###  L265: 
L266: Effective and sustainable use of natural resources with enhanced value addition and management.
L267: 
L268:  L269:

L302:  L303: 
L304: ### 1897 - 1901
L305: 
L306: Railway lines across Kenya, Uganda and Tanganyika open up the region for colonial development under the management of the East African Railways Cooperation.
L307: 
L308:  L309: ### 1900 - 1917
L310: 
L311: Customs for goods destined for Uganda are collected at Mombasa port, and a full Customs Union comprising Kenya, Uganda and, later, Tanganyika is established in 1917.
L312: 
L313:  L314: 
L315: ### 1905 - 1917
L316: 
L317: The East African Currency Board, the Postal Union, the Court of Appeal for Eastern Africa, the East African Governors’ Conference, the East African Income Tax Board and the Joint Economic Council are established.
L318: 
L319:  L320: ### 1946
L321: 
L322: The East African Airways Corporation is incorporated. It covered not only the East African region, but operated across Africa, connecting also to Europe and India.
L323: 
L324:  L325: 
L326: ### 1948 - 1961
L327: 
L328: The East African High Commission (EACH) is the coordinating body to deal with a customs union, a common external tariff, currency and post- age; and also with common services in transport and communications, research and education.
L329: 
L330:  L331: ### 1961
L332: 
L333: Following independence, the East African High Commission is replaced by the East African Common Services Organisation (EACSO), which many observers thought would lead to a political federation between the three territories. However, the new organisation suffered from a lack of joint planning and fiscal policy, separate political policies and Kenya’s dominant economic position.
L334: 
L335:  L336: 
L337: ### 1967
L338: 
L339: Signing of the ‘Treaty for East African Cooperation’ among Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania.
L340: ### 1977
L341: 
L342: Dissolution of the first East African Community.
L343: 
L344:  L345: 
L346: ### 1999
L347: 
L348: The Treaty for the Establishment of the East African Community was signed by the three Presidents of the Republic of Kenya, Republic of Uganda and the United Republic of Tanzania on 30th November, 1999. 30th November is recognized as EAC Day across the region.
L349: 
L350:  L351: 
L352: ### 2005
L353: 
L354: The Protocol for the establishment of the EAC Customs Union was signed on 1st July, 2005.
L355: 
L356:  L357: ### 2007
L358: 
L359: The Republic of Rwanda and the Republic of Burundi were admitted and became full members of EAC on 1st July, 2007.
L360: 
L361:  L362: 
L363: ### 2010
L364: 
L365: The EAC Common Market Protocol entered into force on 1st July, 2010, an expansion of the bloc’s existing Customs Union.
L366: 
L367:  L368: ### 2013
L369: 
L370: The East African Monetary Union (EAMU) is the third milestone of the EAC integration process. The EAMU Protocol was signed on 30th November, 2013 and set the groundwork for a monetary union within 10 years while allowing the EAC Partner States to progressively converge their currencies into a single currency in the Community.
L371: 
L372:  L373: 
L374: ### 2016
L375: 
L376: The Republic of South Sudan was admitted and became a full member of EAC on 5th September, 2016.
L377: 
L378:  L379: ### 2017
L380: 
L381: On 20th May, 2017, the EAC Heads of State adopted the Political Confederation as a transitional model of the East African Political Federation.
L382: 
L383:  L384: 
L385: ### 2022
L386: 
L387: The Democratic Republic of the Congo (DRC), is admited to the EAC and becomes a full member on 11 July, 2022.
L388: 
L389:  L390: 
L391: ### 2023
L392: 
L393: The Federal Republic of Somalia is admited to the EAC and aceedes to the EAC Treaty on 15 December, 2023.
L394: 
L395:  L396: ### 2024
L397: 
L398: The Federal Republic of Somalia deposits her Instrument of Ratification of the EAC Treaty and becomes a full member of the Community on 4 March, 2024
