L27: ## About Tsavo Stepup
L28: 
L29: Welcome to Tsavo Stepup, located on Muthoira Road in the vibrant Riruta neighborhood. This modern residential complex offers 216 units across 12 floors, providing a variety of living spaces to suit different needs. The property features a convenient on-site restaurant and shop, as well as CCTV, borehole, and manned security for a safe and comfortable living environment. Additionally, residents can enjoy the convenience of a laundry mart and the assurance of a manager on-site.
L30: Prospective residents can choose from a selection of thoughtfully designed units, including 1-bedroom apartments and studio options. The 1-bedroom units are ideal for young professionals or couples seeking a cozy and functional living space, while the studio apartments cater to individuals looking for a compact yet comfortable home. All units come with pre-paid electricity, tiled flooring, and an open-plan kitchen, ensuring practicality and ease of maintenance.
L31: With its convenient amenities, secure environment, and a range of unit options, Tsavo Stepup is well-suited for individuals or couples seeking a modern and hassle-free living experience in the heart of Riruta. Whether you're a student, young professional, or small family, this property offers a welcoming and convenient place to call home.
