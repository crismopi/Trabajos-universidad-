L20: March 25, 1980
L21: ## First IDA credit for structural adjustment approved
L22: In order to improve the effectiveness of project lending, the World Bank Group began, in the 1980s, to deal more explicitly with macroeconomic reforms by offering members structural adjustment loans (SALs) and credits. The first SAL credit approved by IDA is for $55 million to Kenya. The credit will provide for a program of economic measures in the areas of foreign trade policy, external debt management, and interest rates and medium-term budgeting.
L23: Meanwhile, foreign exchange will be used for essential imports.
